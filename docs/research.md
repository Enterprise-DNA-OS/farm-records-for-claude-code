# Research notes

Checked 27 September 2026.

## FarmIQ pricing

FarmIQ's packs page lists four packs, priced per farm per month, excluding GST, "Price effective from 1 July 2026":

| Pack | Per month | Per year (x12) |
|---|---|---|
| Lite | NZ$42 | NZ$504 |
| Essentials | NZ$84 | NZ$1,008 |
| Performance+ | NZ$190 | NZ$2,280 |
| Pro | NZ$249 | NZ$2,988 |

Multi-farm: "Get 25% off your second and third farms, and 50% off any farms after that". Unlimited users per farm, month-to-month contracts, no startup costs; onboarding help to draw the farm map is "a small fee". Paid on-site training is available.

Source: https://www.farmiq.co.nz/packs/. The live site refused connections from the build machine on 27 September 2026, so the figures were read from the Internet Archive copy of that page captured 8 June 2026 (https://web.archive.org/web/20260608080235/https://www.farmiq.co.nz/packs/), which shows the 1 July 2026 prices. A search the same day showed the same NZ$249 Pro figure on the live packs page, and the older NZ$235 on the compare-packs page.

This is not a five-figure bill. One farm on Pro is NZ$2,988 a year before GST. The case for switching is owning the records and the rules, not the licence saving.

## What each pack covers (from the same page)

- Lite: phone and desktop app, farm map, health and safety, diary, tasks and plans, staff and contractor records, freshwater farm plan, farm environment plan, NZFAP Plus.
- Essentials adds: automatic NAIT reporting, stock recording (sales, purchases, births, deaths, transfers), animal health treatments, fertiliser and chemical application, weather, paddock history, nutrient application analysis, dashboard, timesheets.
- Performance+ adds: mob-based recording, mob movements, mating, scanning, marking and weaning, liveweights, stock reconciliation, planting and harvest, soil tests, effluent and irrigation, pasture wedge, financial integration, feed planner and budgets, wool and velvet, kill-sheet integration, milk data.
- Pro adds: individual EID recording, sharing animal information between farms, animal history, weight predictor, EID finishing, draft list, Beef EQ reports.

## What this build covers

Mob and individual stock records, NAIT movement and tagging windows, animal health and withholding, liveweights, mating and scanning, sale plans with ASD references, fertiliser and the N cap, the spray diary, hazards and incidents, farm plan actions, tasks and diary. Not covered: the farm map, phone and offline capture, weather, soil tests, effluent and irrigation, feed budgets, wool and velvet, kill-sheet and milk feeds, financial integration, staff timesheets and any live NAIT connection.
